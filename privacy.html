Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026

1. Who We Are

Giant Steps Learning ("we," "our," or "us") is a sole proprietorship operated by its owner in Vernon, British Columbia, Canada. We offer AI literacy education. This policy explains what personal information we collect through giantstepslearning.com and how we handle it. We follow British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

Questions about privacy can be sent to info@giantstepslearning.com. The owner is responsible for our privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

  • Inquiries and emails: When you email us (for example, using the "Send an Inquiry" link or any other email link on the site), we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and anything you write in your message. Our site has no contact form or account system. Email links open your own email program.
  • Workshop participation: If you register for or attend a workshop, we may collect the details needed to run it, such as participant names, contact details for the adult responsible for a participant, and consent forms. We will tell you what we need when you register or book.
  • Technical information: Like most websites, our site is delivered by a hosting provider and by third parties that load content on some pages (see section 4). Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. We do not run our own analytics tool and do not set our own cookies on any page.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To reply to your inquiries and communicate with you about workshops and services
  • To plan, run, and follow up on workshops, including confirming parent or guardian consent
  • To operate and maintain the website
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

We collect, use, and disclose personal information only for purposes a reasonable person would consider appropriate, and with your consent or as PIPA otherwise permits. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services on Our Website

We do not run advertising, analytics, or other third-party services on this site, apart from hosting.

Hosting (all pages)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor information such as IP addresses when you load pages. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

We may link to other sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only with service providers who help us run the business (for example, our email provider), or where required or permitted by law. Some of these providers may store information outside Canada, including in the United States.

6. Children and Young People

Our website is written for adults, including parents, guardians, teachers, and business owners. It is not directed at children, and we ask young people to have a parent or guardian contact us on their behalf.

Our workshops may include teens and other young people. For participants who are minors, we ask for a parent or guardian's consent before they take part, and we collect only the information we need. We handle information about young people carefully, share it only as needed to run the workshop or as the law requires, and do not use it for advertising.

In workshops, participants use third-party AI tools. We ask participants not to type personal information about themselves or others into those tools. Those tools are run by other companies under their own privacy policies and age limits.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information without a parent or guardian's consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Retention and Security

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described above or as the law requires, and then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage over the Internet is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Under PIPA, you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and withdraw your consent to our use of it (subject to legal or contractual limits). To make a request, email info@giantstepslearning.com. We will respond within the time PIPA requires.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
